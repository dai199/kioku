How it works

The loop

One sentence, followed from the moment you look it up to the moment you know it.

01 選ぶSelect

Select text in any app and a small icon appears. Nothing is sent anywhere until you click it — selecting alone never touches the network.

確認して折り返します。
02 置き換えるReplace

Writing in English? Select your Japanese draft and Kioku swaps it for the English you pick, right where you were typing. Ask for a different tone — more casual, more formal, shorter — until it sounds right.

I'll look into it and get back to you.
03 貯まるAccumulate

Every translation is kept locally, with the app you were in and when. This is the part most tools throw away, and it is the part worth keeping.

04 選ばれるCurate

Once a week, an AI reads your history, points out the patterns you keep stumbling on, and proposes a handful of flashcards. You approve the ones you want. Nothing becomes a card without your say-so.

Review with spaced repetition — three ratings, twenty cards a day at most, so a session stays under five minutes.

Privacy

Privacy
Nothing is sent until you click.
Selecting text only brings up an icon. No network request happens.
You can choose to send nothing at all.
Pick Apple's on-device translation and your text never leaves the Mac. It works offline, and costs nothing.
There is no Kioku server.
The API key is yours. Text goes from your Mac straight to the provider you configured.
Some apps are excluded from the start.
Password managers are excluded by default, and you can add any app. In an excluded app the icon never appears.
Your history stays with you.
Translations, cards and reports live in a local database you can inspect or delete yourself.

Install

Install

Kioku is not notarised yet, so there is no prebuilt download. Build it from source instead — you run code you can read, and there is no Gatekeeper warning to work around. macOS 26 and Xcode are required.

Accessibility permission is needed to read the text you select. That same requirement is why Kioku cannot ship on the Mac App Store. Full steps are in the README.
